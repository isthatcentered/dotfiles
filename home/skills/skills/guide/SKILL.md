Guides are located in .agents/guides

The guides index is in .agents/guides/README.md

When creating/updating an existing guide here are the rules to follow:
- Be precise but concise. Use as few words as possible to state your principle, each useless word is an opportunity for confusion.
- Always explain the goal behind a rule/decision, this gives the human/agent the context to decide what to do in unforseen situations
- The guide should be snippet/diagrams driven, a diagram/code snippet usually is more efficient than a 1000 words
- If the existing guide or another guide contradicts the new rule, ask the human what to do (should we update the conflicting guides to match the new rules or should we rethink the rule)
- Section the guide by principle. EX: principle: show only what counts, principle: fix what matters, randomize what doesn't, principle: make the change easy then make the easy change


Don't use a lot of words to describe something that could be better explained with snippets and visualizations. Do better.

**Your are not allowed to modify  a guide without the user's explicit approval or request. If you think a guide should be updated, ask the user and present your case**
